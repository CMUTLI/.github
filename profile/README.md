# Teaching and Learning Innovation at Carnegie Mellon

Software and infrastructure from Carnegie Mellon University's Office of the
Vice Provost for Teaching and Learning Innovation (TLI), which includes the
[Eberly Center for Teaching Excellence and Educational
Innovation](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/), the Core Competencies Initiative,
and CMU Online.

Our tools support CMU instructors and students: course requests and
provisioning for teaching platforms such as Canvas, office hours queues,
syllabus collection, and the servers that run them.

CMU Online's projects are at [github.com/CMUOnline](https://github.com/CMUOnline).
Learn more about TLI at [cmu.edu/tli-office](https://www.cmu.edu/tli-office/).
